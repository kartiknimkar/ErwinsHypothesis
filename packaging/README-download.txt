ERWIN'S HYPOTHESIS
==================

Nothing to install. Everything the app needs is inside this folder.

WINDOWS
  1. Unzip this folder somewhere on your own disk (e.g. Documents), not a network drive.
  2. Double-click ErwinsHypothesis.exe.
  3. The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click
     "More info", then "Run anyway". (The app isn't code-signed; this is normal.)

MAC (Apple Silicon: M1/M2/M3/M4)
  1. Unzip, and move the "Erwins Hypothesis" folder to Documents (or anywhere
     outside Downloads).
  2. Double-click ErwinsHypothesis.app. If macOS says it can't be checked,
     open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".

YOUR FILES
  Projects are saved in the "projects" folder next to the app, and exported
  graphs and Excel files in the "exports" folder. To keep your work when you
  update, copy the "projects" folder into the new version's folder.
  To remove the app completely, delete this folder.
